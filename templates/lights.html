Network DMX — Art-Net & sACN

Browsers cannot send UDP, so these go through a bridge you run on this computer. It is one file, standard library only, and it forwards finished DMX packets to your lighting network — it never sees your audio or your show.

Download bridge

To run it: python lx-bridge.py — add --artnet-host 192.168.1.55 to aim at a specific node, or --port to move it. Art-Net goes to UDP 6454, sACN to 5568, and the universe number comes from the rig settings above.

Rig profile

A rig is the room: how many bars, what they are, where they hang, how they're patched. Your cues are looks and group roles, not channel numbers, so the same show re-renders on any rig — bar-specific cues move to the matching position instead of going dark.
