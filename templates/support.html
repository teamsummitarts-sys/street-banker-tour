Support

Support and account requests

For help with Street Banker, email our support team. Please do not send passwords, card details, or unreleased audio by email.

support@streetbankermusic.com

Account and access

Use password recovery if you cannot sign in. Include the email on the account and a short description of the issue.

Billing and subscriptions

If you can sign in, review your billing settings there. If access is blocked or a charge needs review, email Support with the account email and the relevant date or invoice reference.

Projects, links, and metadata

For help with audio projects, smart links, metadata, or a product workflow, tell us what you were trying to do, the page you were on, and any error message you saw.

Privacy and data requests

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data. We may need to verify your account before we act on a request.

Helpful information to include

Your account email, the product or page involved, what happened, and the best way to reach you. Please do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or private credentials.
