11 Metadata Passport + Rights

One release.Every detail connected.

Organise credits, splits, identifiers, ownership, agreements, assets, versions and release history in one living record.

Open Metadata Passport

Example metadata passport

01 Credits

Needs Review
What is stored
Primary artist · Featured artist · Songwriters · Producers · Engineers · Musicians · Photographers · Designers · Additional contributors
Why it matters
Credits are how a person gets paid and how they get found. A contributor with no role attached is invisible to every system downstream.
Missing information
Two performers are listed without a role.
Recommended action
Assign a role to every contributor before the release is confirmed.

02 Ownership

Conflicts Detected
What is stored
Master owner · Composition owners · Publishing shares · Administration · Territories · Ownership percentages · Effective dates
Why it matters
Splits that do not add up are the most common reason money sits uncollected. Ownership is also the first thing anybody reviewing the catalog asks for.
Missing information
Composition shares total 90%.
Recommended action
Reconcile the composition split with the writers named in Credits, then have it confirmed.

03 Identifiers

Needs Review
What is stored
ISRC · UPC or EAN · ISWC · IPI or CAE · ISNI · Catalog number · Internal release ID
Why it matters
Identifiers connect the release to platforms, registrations, statements and royalty sources. Incorrect or missing identifiers fragment reporting.
Missing information
ISWC and two contributor IPI numbers have not been added.
Recommended action
Confirm the composition registration and complete contributor identifiers before final delivery.

04 Versions

Incomplete
What is stored
Original · Clean · Explicit · Instrumental · Acapella · Radio edit · Extended · Live · Remix · Alternate master
Why it matters
Each version is its own recording with its own identifier. A version without a matching asset cannot be delivered, and a version delivered without its own ISRC splits the reporting.
Missing information
The clean version has no audio asset attached.
Recommended action
Attach the clean master or remove the version from the plan.

05 Agreements

Incomplete
What is stored
Split sheets · Producer agreements · Featured-artist agreements · Work-for-hire agreements · Master-use licences · Sample clearances · Publishing administration · Distribution agreements
Why it matters
An agreement is what turns a claim into a right. Without the paperwork behind a split, the split is an intention.
Missing information
The split sheet has not been signed by one writer.
Recommended action
Send the split sheet for the outstanding signature.

06 Assets

Ready for Confirmation
What is stored
Audio masters · Stems · Artwork · Artist photography · Lyrics · Videos · Canvas clips · Campaign files · Press materials · Supporting documents
Why it matters
The files are the release. Keeping them against the record rather than in a folder somewhere is what makes a re-issue, a sync request or a transfer possible years later.
Missing information
Nothing outstanding.
Recommended action
Confirm the master and artwork as final.

07 Release History

Complete
What is stored
Original release · Metadata updates · Artwork changes · New territories · Clean version added · Remix added · Deluxe version · Distribution transfer · Rights corrections · Catalog ownership updates
Why it matters
Catalog value is a paper trail. What changed, when and on whose authority is the difference between a catalog somebody can buy and one nobody can price.
Missing information
Nothing outstanding.
Recommended action
Keep recording changes as they happen.

A songwriter is added under Credits.

  • OwnershipThe composition split has to be reopened and the new share agreed.
  • IdentifiersTheir IPI or CAE number is needed, or the registration cannot name them.
  • AgreementsThe split sheet has to be reissued and signed.
  • VersionsEvery version of the recording inherits the change.
  • Release HistoryThe correction is recorded, with the date and who made it.

One entry, five consequences. That is why this is a record rather than a form.
