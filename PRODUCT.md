# Street Banker Company OS

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent artists and artist-led companies operating releases, rights, revenue,
campaigns, production, and touring without a conventional full-time staff.

## Product Purpose

Street Banker V2 gives an artist an accountable ten-person digital music
company. The artist gives the Manager an objective; the Manager turns it into
assigned work, deadlines, specialist handoffs, deliverables, and approvals.
Success means the artist can see what must happen next, who owns it, and what
finished output was produced.

## Positioning

One objective enters through the Manager and becomes coordinated, visible work
across nine specialist desks. Insights are not the end product; accountable
execution and stored deliverables are.

## Operating Context

The core operating loop is Objective -> Plan -> Assigned work -> Specialist
deliverables -> Owner approval. The Manager is always the first signed-in
surface. Existing V2 modules supply tools to specialist desks, but the Company
OS is the primary navigation and coordination layer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Exactly ten permanent digital team members: Manager, A&R, Marketer,
  Publicist, Tour Manager, Royalty Accountant, Rights Attorney, Creative
  Director, Producer, and Executive Assistant.
- Every team member has exactly two named core capabilities.
- Work, deadlines, statuses, blockers, deliverables, approvals, and history are
  owner-scoped stored records; the interface must not claim actions occurred
  when no record exists.
- Human collaborators are separate from the ten digital desks.
- Registration is closed for the comparison build. Only the existing approved
  owner account may sign in; no public or demo accounts are created in the
  deployed environment.
- V2 must never share code changes, deployments, databases, storage, secrets,
  or service configuration with V1.
- Rights guidance identifies issues and prepares materials; it is not legal
  representation.
- The current V2 Render service uses ephemeral storage until the owner
  authorizes paid durable infrastructure.

## Brand Commitments

Street Banker is authoritative, direct, premium, and music-industry specific.
The signed-in Company OS uses a dense black and warm-brass control-room
language, inspired by the approved Tour, Studio, and Remix Lab references.
It must not become a generic SaaS dashboard, a collection of oversized cards,
or a duplicate of the public V1 site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing V2 repository and working specialist modules.
- Approved Tour, Studio, and Remix Lab interface references supplied by the
  owner.
- Current Team prototype and its objective ledger, which are implementation
  evidence but not visual or product authority.
- No public performance, revenue, or customer claims may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The Manager leads; specialists execute.
2. Every status must be backed by stored work.
3. One objective creates a coordinated plan, not ten disconnected chats.
4. Deliverables and approvals matter more than passive analytics.
5. V2 remains operationally and technically isolated from V1.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, reduced-motion support,
WCAG-AA text contrast, and responsive mobile operation are required.
