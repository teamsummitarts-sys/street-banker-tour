Privacy & session data

Your source audio is processed in this browser's memory. This module does not upload audio or save recordings in browser storage. Use only audio you own or have permission to process.

Create sound sends the typed description to OpenAI through V2's authenticated server. The provider does not hear the audio. Noise Lab does not save descriptions or provider response bodies, and requests response storage off. OpenAI may retain content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, with legal exceptions; zero retention has not been verified for this account.

The server keeps account-linked attempt counts, success/failure counts, token totals when reported, and total generation time in memory until restart. They have no automatic backup and are used to enforce the pilot allowance. Clear session does not reset that allowance or delete provider logs. Cancelled or failed requests may still incur provider charges.

Closing or reloading the page clears this session. Returning through browser history preserves it only if the browser kept this page in memory. Unsaved audio and edits have no automatic recovery. WAV and recipe files you download remain wherever you save them; delete those files from your device or backup service when you no longer want them.

When private saving is enabled, patch names and versioned settings are stored in your V2 account until you delete the patch. Audio and sound descriptions are not saved with it. Only your account can use the patch API; authorized V2 operators can access the database and its backups. Clear session leaves saved patches intact. Delete patch removes all its versions from the active library. Minimal request identifiers and hashes remain to prevent duplicate saves, without patch names or recipes. Account saving is limited to 50 patches, 100 versions per patch and 5,000 recorded save requests. Downloads and backup copies are separate: deletion does not erase copies you or an operator already exported. Render may keep disk snapshots for at least seven days; no maximum deletion time is verified. Database recovery uses operator-created consistent backups, not disk snapshots. No scheduled off-site backup or recovery guarantee has been verified. Keep an exported copy of valued patches.

Browser support & playback

Checking this browser's audio capabilities…

Use Play to enable audio. Mobile browsers may pause when you lock the screen, switch apps, or change audio devices. Return here and press Play to resume. Keep this page in the foreground for the pilot.

Imports support mono or stereo RIFF WAV: PCM 16/24/32-bit or 32-bit float, 8–192 kHz, up to 30 seconds and 20 MiB. MP3, compressed WAV, and WAV extensible are unsupported. Import uses basic linear resampling and short loop-edge fades; this is not an archival audio converter. A failed replacement keeps your previous source.

Hardware, live instrument input, and universal pedal export are outside this prototype. Device and sustained-playback validation is still pending. Capability detection is not a claim of tested device support. Start at a comfortable listening volume.

Controls & recipe versions

Tab to each control. Use arrow keys on sliders, or enter an exact value. Home and End move to a slider's limits. Each continuous slider adjustment is one undo step.

A auditions the previous patch. B returns to your current patch. Compare clean bypasses effects independently. Controls are locked while auditioning A; switch to B to continue editing. Downloads use the current B recipe, even while comparing. Changing the source or settings during a WAV render cancels that render.

Space controls filtered echo. The optional export tail is fixed at two seconds, so a longer echo may be cut short. Recipe files include their schema and engine version. Unsupported or invalid recipes are rejected, keeping the current working patch. Downloaded recipes contain settings only; keep source audio separately to recreate a sound.
